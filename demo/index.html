Detecting learner states from gaze and pen data

In my research, participants read short English passages and wrote their answers with a digital pen while a Tobii eye tracker recorded where they looked. After each question, they reported what they had mainly been doing:

LocatingSearching the passage for the relevant part
UnderstandingWorking out what the text means
ReasoningCombining information to form an answer

I then trained machine-learning models to predict these states from gaze and handwriting features. This page is a public demo of the task interface used in the study.

Demo mode. This version runs entirely in your browser. There is no server, nothing you write is saved or sent anywhere, and no eye tracker is needed. You can write with a mouse, trackpad, touchscreen or stylus.

What you will do

  1. Read one short sample passage.
  2. Answer three sub-questions by writing in the answer box.
  3. After each one, fill in the short questionnaire that participants used to label their state.
